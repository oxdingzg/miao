# miao v0.2.18

This release makes Remote Control account-based. Sign in to the same Hub account on the computer and iOS, then enable a Session on the computer. The first connection does not require a pairing code, QR scan, device approval, or signer-roster initialization.

## Remote Control

- `/login` signs the computer into its Hub account. In an open Session, `/remote-control` silently toggles sharing for that Session. Enter it again to stop sharing; other shared Sessions remain available.
- iOS automatically discovers online account computers and lists their shared Sessions in the drawer. A Session can be opened and operated from the phone, including rename, agent/model selection, queued prompts, and permission replies.
- Account authorization uses the identity attested by the authenticated Hub control connection, plus client key-possession proof and an encrypted channel. A client-supplied account ID alone cannot authorize access. Unshared Sessions are denied, and stopping sharing invalidates subsequent operations immediately.
- This account mode requires an updated computer and iOS client, and a Hub that forwards the authenticated account ID to the host. Old independent device grants remain supported but are no longer required by the iOS account flow.

## iOS recovery

- Restore account connections and retained drafts after a cold launch.
- Reuse stable host/client identity while refreshing the directory, rather than discarding every account host when a transient identity lookup fails.
- Cache verified identity only within the current login generation, clearing it on sign-out/account replacement.
- Use page-based Agent/model/variant pickers with explicit accessible selection values.
