## [0.2.11] - 2026-10-16

miao 0.2.11 is a small release: two additions to remote access and iOS builds,
and three fixes, including one that made a long session's transcript look blank
after tool output collapsed.

### Added

- **Remote access**: the scope and duration of a shared session are chosen when
  access is granted, and one default applies to all sessions (#878).
- **iOS**: TestFlight builds are published from a manual workflow (#884).

### Fixed

- **Transcript**: a transcript whose tail was partially covered no longer goes
  blank when tool output collapses; the anchor is protected only when the cover
  is complete (#889).
- **Sessions (iOS)**: a relay 404 reports an unsupported-feature message instead
  of a network failure (#877).
- **Engine**: an attempt notice no longer counts as the turn's first output, so a
  turn that produced nothing keeps its transport retries, and a sweep reads every
  attempt as `providerAttempts` (#890).

### Validation and upgrade

- Upgrade through the usual miao auto-update channel, or use the repository
  installer. The published artifact is smoke-tested by launching the terminal
  interface, not only by asking it for its version.
